## Clearance timelines

`estimate_timeline` reads `data/timelines.json` (last verified 2026-11-12): the Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency's most recent published quarterly averages, by level, plus modifiers for an employer with or without a facility clearance and for an existing, interim or lapsed clearance. A quarterly average is a mean, not a median. Where no primary source publishes a number, the tool returns none. For a non-citizen it returns the citizenship rule, not a day count. The dataset is also the resource `cleared://timelines.json`.

## The recruiter guide

The page [`/docs/recruiter`](https://cleared-mcp.vercel.app/docs/recruiter) is **available**, but most of what it describes is **not built yet**. It walks through the 38 steps from opening a role to day one, generated from `data/pipeline-steps.json`: what happens, what cleared would do, what a named person must do and why, and what must never happen before what, with every legal point linked to its source and date. Each part that is not built is marked "Not built yet". The parts that work today are the `explain_rule` topics it links to.

## How sources stay honest

- `SOURCES.md` and the `/sources` page list every source. The parts drawn from the data files are printed by `scripts/generate-sources.ts`.
- `tests/unit/data-integrity.test.ts` fails if a URL cited in the data, or by a tool, is missing from `SOURCES.md` or `/sources`.
- `pnpm linkcheck` opens every cited URL.
- When a source and the product disagree, the product changes. Three timeline ranges that no cited source contained were removed this way.

None of this is legal advice. Verify anything that matters with the employer's Facility Security Officer or a lawyer.
