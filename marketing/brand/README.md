## The phone's screens

The phone on the page shows Grok Bot's or Muse's own mobile interface, redrawn by the page in `components/stage/screen.ts`. Layouts follow the apps' App Store screenshots and recordings of the apps in use. Where neither showed a state, it is drawn in the app's pattern. Every word of content on those screens is from `lib/copy.ts`. Every state can be seen in both apps at `/screens`, which is not linked or indexed.

## Rules

- Use each mark exactly as provided: no recolouring, redrawing, stretching or combining. See the [x.ai brand guidelines](https://x.ai/legal/brand-guidelines) and the Meta Brand Resource Center.
- Draper's logo is not used, because its brand guide requires written approval. The page sets the name in type instead.
- Replace these with files from the companies' official asset packs when they are available.
